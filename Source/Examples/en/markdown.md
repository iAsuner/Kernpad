# Bring reading back into the week.

**PROJECT DESK**

Quiet Reading is a five-day plan, currently in progress.

Turn a pile of saved articles into a reading list you actually want to open. Keep twenty minutes each evening: read one piece, write three sentences, and leave yourself a new question.

## 01 / Do one thing well

> Collect a little less. Understand a little more. Leave each reading with a thought of your own.

**The finish line**: ten selected articles, three themes, and one page of personal conclusions. Keep the material on your device so you can continue away from a connection.

- Select
  - Keep the pieces you want to read and remove duplicate saves
  - Write **one sentence** explaining why each piece belongs
- Read
  - Separate ideas you agree with from those you still question
  - Keep your questions in `reading.md`; resist copying an answer too soon

## 02 / From intention to something finished

| When | Focus | What to keep |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monday | Sort and select | Ten articles across three themes |
| Tuesday–Wednesday | Read and annotate | Three sentences: idea, evidence, question |
| Thursday | Compare the ideas | One comparison table and a new understanding |
| Friday | Reflect and share | A one-page summary exported to PDF |

## 03 / Give the habit a little structure

```json
{
  "project": "Quiet Reading",
  "minutesPerDay": 20,
  "mode": "offline",
  "capture": ["idea", "evidence", "question"]
}
```

Keep configuration in a code block, where indentation and punctuation stay intact. Put the author's ideas in the body, mark **your own conclusions** in bold, and leave *open questions* in italics for another day.
