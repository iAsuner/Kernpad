04 / Principles for a useful page

  • Lead with the conclusion. The heading sets a direction; the body explains the reason.
  • Give color a purpose. The main color carries information. Accents identify what matters most.
  • Leave room to read. Align pictures and text. Divide long passages into sections worth finishing.
